Who We Are

YOU & Me Siraj Organisation is a community-based charity founded with one goal — to serve those in need. We visit children's homes, provide food and clothing, spread Islamic knowledge and work together to build a better community.

Our name says it all — it's not just me, it's YOU and ME together making a difference.
